Never spend tomorrow’s payroll on today’s expenses.

Your business’s money stays in a treasury you own on NEAR. Its agent, Banto, sets money aside for every bill you accept and pays only inside the rules you sign. It can never take the money, and you can switch it off at any time.

When a plan lapses

A lapse never locks your money.

Takara is non-custodial. It never holds your funds, so it can’t hold them back. These rules are absolute.

  • Your treasuryThe treasury is yours. Funds are always withdrawable, even with our servers, the enclave and Banto all switched off. Check 8 proves it.
  • Your recordsCommitments, vendors, receipts and history are always exportable, continuously, not on request.
  • What stopsBanto stops taking invoices, authorizing payments and sending alerts.
  • Grace periodPayroll and anything marked critical still settles for 14 days after the lapse. Everything else freezes immediately.
  • WarningsAt 14, 7 and 1 days, each naming exactly which bills will go unpaid.

Verify it yourself

Don’t take this page’s word for it. Each command reads the chain or checks an attestation from your own machine.

Read the live treasury

No keys needed. Prints owners, signers, the agent, the policy hash and the pinned engine build.

git clone https://github.com/growthmindsetgg/takara-ai.git
cd takara-ai && npm install
node -e 'import("./scripts/lib/near.mjs").then(async m=>console.log(await m.view("treasury.takara-ai-hq.testnet","get_config")))'

Run all ten checks on a treasury you own

Deploys a fresh treasury to your testnet account, runs the checks as real transactions and starts the local owner dashboard. About 20 minutes.

TAKARA_OWNER=<you>.testnet node scripts/setup-testnet.mjs
node scripts/judge.mjs
node backend/server.mjs   # owner dashboard on localhost:8787
