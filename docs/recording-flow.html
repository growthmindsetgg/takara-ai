TAKARA AI · HACKATHON DEMO

Recording flow, shot by shot

About 3 minutes. Record on NEAR Mainnet at takara-ai.vercel.app. Figures below are what the app showed at seeding; read them off the screen when you record.

Preflight, before you press record

  1. Open takara-ai.vercel.app/app. The footer says Live from NEAR mainnet (or add ?network=mainnet).
  2. Optional, to show the tour: open the browser console, run
    localStorage.removeItem("takara.tour.v2.main")
    and reload.
  3. Sidebar foot: Viewing as: Owner. Close Ask Banto.
  4. Fresh window, 1280 × 800 or larger, no extensions on the page.
  5. Signer wallet (signer.takara-ai-hq.near) ready in Meteor or MyNearWallet. Popups allowed.
  6. Terminal open in the repo for shot 15.

Rules

  • Reject the Bigagency bill as the Signer. Shots 9 and 10. Type a reason, confirm in the wallet.
  • Never press "Approve anyway". It would leave the treasury 0.825 USDC short for everyone.
  • Don't click the gear icon in the wallet chooser. It opens a developer settings screen.
  • Don't say "no tokens move". Settled bills are real USDC transfers on mainnet.
Retake? Once Bigagency is rejected it's closed. Run NEAR_NETWORK=mainnet node scripts/seed-demo.mjs --execute for a fresh bill.
